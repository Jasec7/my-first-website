Who's Jasec Rangel?

Jasec Rangel is a dedicated professional from Caracas, Venezuela. His career in the culinary arts began at the age of 19 when, after completing high school, he immediately enrolled in culinary school to pursue his dream of becoming a chef. Upon graduating, Jasec gained valuable experience working for a year at a small bistro, where he honed his foundational skills.He continued his journey by briefly working for a national cruise line, followed by positions at two other restaurants, where he expanded his expertise by taking on various roles and responsibilities. Seeking to enhance his personal and professional growth, Jasec traveled to San Francisco, CA, to study English. After completing his language course, he successfully transitioned into the local culinary scene, working as a cook. After a few years, Jasec decided to explore bartending as a new career path, driven by the potential for better income. This decision proved to be both enjoyable and educational, allowing him to further develop his customer service and communication skills. Through bartending, Jasec gained deeper knowledge of beverages, food, and diverse cultures, enriching his professional and personal life.
